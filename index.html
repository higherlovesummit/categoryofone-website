Why Branding

Now, more than ever, your brand is your advantage.

We're living through the most significant shift in modern business history. AI has accelerated what's possible, and in the process, made everything start to look, sound, and feel the same.

Over five million new businesses launched last year. Content is created faster than anyone can consume it. And in the process, trust is dissolving. People no longer ask, "Is this good?" They ask, "Is this real?"

In a market this crowded, the answer isn't louder marketing. It's deeper resonance. No matter how advanced AI becomes, it can't replicate human connection. Stop competing in a category of many, and become a Category of One brand.

Our Founder

Re Perez

Re Perez is the Founder & CEO of Category of One Branding and a well-respected brand strategist with 25+ years of experience. He doesn't just build brands; he helps successful brands become more successful. Across more than 1,000 brand transformations for impact-driven CEOs and founder-led B2B companies, Re has developed a proven methodology for elevating brands by blending enterprise-level branding, A.I., and human optimization.

Before stepping into entrepreneurship in 2010, he cut his teeth at some of the top global brand consultancies in New York City, advising Fortune 500 companies including Abbott Nutrition, GE Money, Lilly, Novo Nordisk, TD Ameritrade, and Xerox. Today he is also a sought-after speaker, sharing forward-thinking insights and actionable strategies at events such as the Baby Bathwater Institute, CADRECon, EO's Global Leadership Conference, SellerCon, Traffic & Conversion Summit, and Ontrapalooza.
