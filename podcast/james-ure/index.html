Episode 001

How Authenticity & Alignment Are the Real Mission in Business (and Life)

James Ure·Founder, Mojo Village·Category of One with Re Perez

About This Episode

There are moments in our lives when the deeper truth inside us asks for space. This conversation with my friend James Ure opened that doorway in a powerful way. In this inaugural episode, we talk about the quiet awakenings that show up in midlife, the way energetic alignment shapes every decision, and what happens when you choose a life that feels true to who you are.

You'll hear James share the journey that led him away from a company he spent 14 years building, the experiment that has shaped his last decade, and the clarity that inspired his new venture, Mojo Village. A grounded conversation about embodiment, truth, and the freedom that rises when you choose the life you want to create.

In This Episode

  • Energetic Alignment — Your body's signals show where life wants to move you.
  • Freedom Through Self-Honesty — Life clears when you honor what's true for you.
  • The Midlife Activation — A rising energy in your forties calls for deeper truth.
  • Created Life — Fulfillment grows through conscious, daily intention.
  • The Power of Self-Love — Embracing your essence strengthens your leadership.
  • Identity as Foundation — Your brand stands apart when it mirrors your truth.
