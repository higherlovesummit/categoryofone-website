Episode 008

“I Came Out at 42, Married, With Kids” — A Founder’s Journey Beyond Fear

Alon Ozery·Entrepreneur, Author & Coach·Category of One with Re Perez

About This Episode

There are moments in life when the truth stops waiting patiently. It asks to be lived, even when doing so puts everything you've built at risk. I sit down with Alon Ozery to talk about what it means to come out later in life while married with children — the years of denial, the fear of losing his family, and the relief that followed telling the truth.

Alon is an entrepreneur, former co-CEO of a successful North American bakery, author of “Even the Sidewalk Could Tell,” and now an energy guide and coach. We explore how coming out at 42 reshaped his relationship with his kids, his leadership, his connection to money, and his willingness to live in alignment with who he actually is.

In This Episode

  • Truth Surfacing — The quiet realization that the life he built no longer matched who he was.
  • Embodied Honesty — What changed the moment he finally said the words out loud.
  • Leadership Evolution — How coming out reshaped his confidence, leadership, and relationship to power.
  • Family Redefined — The hardest fear he carried about his kids — and what actually happened.
  • Energetic Integrity — Why hiding drained him, and what became possible once that energy returned.
  • Freedom Lived — What life, work, and self-trust look like on the other side of the truth.
