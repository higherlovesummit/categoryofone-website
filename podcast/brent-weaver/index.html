Episode 010

Why Agencies Get Stuck — and How to Become the Obvious Choice

Brent Weaver·CEO, E2M Solutions·Category of One with Re Perez

About This Episode

What happens when your company has outgrown its brand, but the market still sees an earlier version of you? I'm joined by my longtime friend and client Brent Weaver, CEO of E2M Solutions — a 300+ person white-label services company serving digital agencies across WordPress, eCommerce, SEO, and AI-powered solutions.

Brent shares the emotional moment when E2M's internal evolution no longer matched its external expression, and how adopting a Category of One mindset reshaped confidence, positioning, and decision-making across the business.

In This Episode

  • Brand as Perception Framework — Shapes trust before sales conversations.
  • Clarity Reduces Leadership Drag — Decision-making becomes lighter and faster.
  • Consistency Builds Market Trust — Alignment reinforces credibility over time.
  • Category Recognition Matters — Buyers need a mental container for who you are.
  • Embodied Branding Fuels Confidence — Leaders show up anchored and certain.
  • Innovation Signals Category Leadership — Actions reinforce differentiation.
“A Category of One brand is built on a personal philosophy, not a marketing plan.”Re Perez
