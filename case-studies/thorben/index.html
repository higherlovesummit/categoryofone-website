Case Study · Government IT & Technical Consulting

Thorben

Giving a federal-trusted, woman-owned firm the brand authority to match its work.

The Challenge

Thorben, a 100% woman-owned business delivering cloud, ERP, and technical consulting services, had a strong pedigree serving clients like the U.S. Department of the Interior, the Treasury Department, and the Department of Homeland Security. But its branding gave the appearance of a scrappy upstart rather than an established firm at the top of its game.

The Transformation

We elevated the brand to carry the authority of a top-tier consultancy - a new identity system, messaging, and digital presence that accurately exuded who the company had become.

The Result

Within weeks of launching the new brand, Thorben secured a $1M contract.
