Case Study · Craft Spirits & Distilling

BENDT Distilling Co.

Transforming old-school, pretentious whiskey tradition into something refreshing and welcoming.

5 Brands
One Architecture Built To Scale

The Challenge

BENDT Distilling Co. makes authentic Texas whiskey in Lewisville - but it competes in a category that sells itself on pretense. Heritage crests. Ornate scripts. A tone that tells the newcomer they do not belong.

BENDT’s own brand had inherited that language. The wordmark buried the product, the template website did nothing to separate it from a hundred other craft distilleries, and a growing ready-to-drink line had nowhere in the system to live.

The Transformation

We rebuilt BENDT on a plain-spoken idea the company could actually stand behind: transforming old-school, pretentious whiskey traditions into unique, refreshing, and welcoming whiskey experiences.

A confident modern wordmark replaced the ornament. A navy, cream, and red system carried it across bottle, can, coaster, and apparel. And a brand architecture gave No5, Mule, Sour, and Old Fashioned each their own identity while keeping every one of them unmistakably BENDT.

The Result

A house of brands that scales from a 750ml bottle of American blended whiskey to a canned cocktail without diluting the parent - and a brand as welcoming as the whiskey it sells.
