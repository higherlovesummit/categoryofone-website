Case Study

Extraordinary Life

A stale brand replaced to match the depth of a groundbreaking company.

ResultNew name, identity & website

The Challenge

Dr. Al Spicer’s boutique executive coaching company served visionary leaders focused on life, leadership, and legacy - but years-old branding no longer reflected the depth and quality of the work. Potential clients perceived less than the company delivered.

The Transformation

We executed a complete rebrand: a new name, Extraordinary Life, a new Brand Identity, a new website, and a clear direction for the business.

The Result

The disconnect closed - a brand that finally matches the caliber of the company behind it.
