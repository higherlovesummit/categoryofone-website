Case Study

Odyssey Test Prep

Repositioning a founder-built tutoring service into a premium law-school partner.

The Challenge

Odyssey Test Prep delivered elite LSAT results, but behind the outcomes sat a DIY brand the founder had built himself. The original identity and website couldn't attract or enroll the high-caliber students who would benefit most from the company's level of service. The brand was capping the credibility of the program.

The Transformation

We rebuilt the identity to match the caliber of the results, repositioning Odyssey from a tutoring service into a premium law-school partner. The new brand system - identity, messaging, website, and collateral - was engineered to scale beyond the founder.

The Result

Revenue has grown nearly 10x since the rebrand. Within the first month of launch, the company grew 399% - and continued topping its previous month thereafter.
